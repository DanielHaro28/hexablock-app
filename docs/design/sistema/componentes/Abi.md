# Abi

Abi, the little bee, is the suite's mascot, drawn in a 4 px ink line over a honey-hexagon blob.

**Provide:** `pose` (`wave`, `celebrate`, `cup`), `size` (112 in dialogs; 240–280 on home and loading), `blob` to draw the coloured hexagon behind her.

- Waving: home and tutorial, `sky` blob. Celebrating: victory, big combos, records, `lilac` blob with petals and stars. Hugging a cup: defeat, pause and «sin conexión», `mint` blob, eyes closed.
- Round butter body, two soft ink stripes, cream wings with a sky glint, rose-tipped antennae. No stinger. Never redraw her: use these poses or the SVGs in Mascota.
